Spin meta-GGA: pin the reference values first, and record what libxc cannot provide

scripts/pyscf_spin_mgga_reference.py emits spin_mgga_pointwise.csv: 5 samples
(closed shell, weakly polarised, fully polarised, sparse, large gradient) x 4
components, each row carrying e, v_rho_alpha/beta, the three d e/d sigma
channels and d e/d tau_alpha/beta, with source_doi/source_url so it joins the
DA-08 family list (gate now reports 15 family files, 1150 rows).

The probing also settled what the external ruler can and cannot cover in this
build: MGGA_X_M06 and MGGA_X_M06_2X do not exist in this libxc (KeyError);
only MGGA_X_M06_L and the four M06 correlations are available. So the pinned
reference covers the correlation side - the actually hard half, since M06
correlation needs the zeta-dependent Stoll decomposition - while the exchange
half has exact spin scaling e_sigma = e_closed(2 rho_sigma, 4 sigma,
2 tau_sigma) and can be verified point by point against our own closed-shell
implementation.

Also recorded in the ledger: PySCF returns meta-GGA derivatives as
(exc, [v_rho, v_sigma, v_lapl, v_tau], None, None) - a list inside out[1], not
out[2]/out[3]; reading it by intuition gives a TypeError.
