Summary and Conclusions
Vibrational eﬀects cannot be ignored in the case of 13C
isotropic HFCC for CH 3
Compared to other averaging methods, our averaging
included the rotational motion and coupling between
vibrational modes
With temperature eﬀects, the ro-vibrational contribution
will exceed the electronic contribution
